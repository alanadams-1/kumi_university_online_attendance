ID card maker

Turn a student list into printable ID cards with a QR code on the back. Everything happens in your browser, and no student data is uploaded anywhere.

1. Paste your student list

Columns, in this order: Surname, Given name, Sex (M or F), Registration number, Faculty, Course and, if you have it, Card number. You can paste straight from Excel or Google Sheets, or load a CSV file.

2. Card settings

Using the Kumi University logo. Reset

This tool lays out the card and its QR code. Photos, signatures and the final card printing stay with the university's ID office.
